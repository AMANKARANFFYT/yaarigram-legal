Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 What we collect: your username, name, bio, profile photo, posts, reels, stories, comments, likes, follows, chat messages, voice messages, optional notes and avatar, last-active time, and call signalling data that you create in the app. We do not ask for your email, phone number or contacts. How it is used: only to run the app (show your content to other users, deliver messages and notifications, show reports to the admin, moderate the community). We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. AI features: text you send in the Aman AI chats (and image requests) is sent to Google Gemini to create replies. Do not share sensitive information there. GIF search uses Giphy. Where it is stored: Google Firebase (account login and database) and Cloudinary (reel videos). Voice and video calls go directly between users using WebRTC. Permissions: camera and microphone (photos, reels, calls, voice messages), notifications (messages, likes, follows), storage/gallery (choose media). The app runs a background service only to deliver message notifications. Community safety: users can report, block, restrict and mute accounts. Admins may warn, deactivate or ban accounts that break the rules and remove content. Your control: you can edit your profile, make it private, block or restrict people, and delete your account from Settings > Delete account, which removes your account, posts, reels and stories. You can also request deletion at: DELETE_ACCOUNT page / by email below. Children: the app is for people aged 13 and over. Contact: AMANKARANYTFF, amankaranytff@gmail.com (in-app admin @amankaranytff).
